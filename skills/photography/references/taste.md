# Photography taste

What strong photography of people and places looks like, in the documentary and editorial work brands
commission. It describes; it never decides: the brand's direction and its style block decide the
look. A mockup's shot of the brand's objects has its own page: `references/taste.md` of the
`mockup` skill.

## Strong

- **A moment, not a pose.** People caught doing the thing the brand is about, in the middle of it;
  the picture could only have been taken a second before or after by luck.
- **One idea per frame,** readable at phone size: one action, one place, one feeling.
- **A real place with its wear.** Scuffed floors, a chipped mug, handwritten notes, the light of
  that room. The place says where the brand lives.
- **Light from the scene,** with a direction: a window, a lamp, the low sun. Shadows fall one way and
  the darkest tones still hold colour.
- **The camera where a person would be:** at eye or table height, at a distance that feels like
  being there, rarely from above or very low without a reason.
- **People who look like the brand's customers and staff,** in their own clothes, unstyled, with
  real skin and hair.
- **Space kept on purpose** for the words, as part of the picture: a wall, a sky, a counter's edge.

## Weak, and why it happens

- **Everyone smiling at the camera:** the prompt asked for people, not a moment. Give them a task.
- **The product held up beside a face:** an advertising pose, never a moment. Show it in use.
- **A laptop, a mug and a plant on a white desk:** the generic picture of work. Use the brand's own
  tools and place.
- **Perfect symmetry, even light, a blurred background everywhere:** stock defaults; place the camera
  and the light.
- **A crowd of props:** the model fills a scene it wasn't given. Name the few the moment uses.
- **Hands doing nothing in the middle of the frame:** no job was given. Give each hand one, or crop it.

## Defaults to recognise

The image model's default person is young, slim, smiling and lit from the front, in a clean room with
a plant. When the picture comes out like that, the plan left the person and the place open: name who
they are, where they are and what they're doing.
