# Character taste

What a strong brand character looks like, from a study of current identity work. It describes; it
never decides: the brief and the chosen direction decide who the character is and how it's drawn.
The logo's own page is `references/taste.md` of the `logo` skill; the families of rendering are in
`styles.md`.

## Strong

- **A personality in the pose and the face:** a grin, a wink, a run, a lean, a slurp.
- **One feature that makes it itself:** a thing it wears, a marking, a habit.
- **Doing this business's things:** carrying the product, serving it, eating it, making it.
- **Shown as a set:** the same character across poses and expressions proves it's a character and
  not one drawing; its sheet is that proof.
- **On model in every pose:** the same face, hair, clothes, proportions and line from pose to pose.
- **Reads as a profile picture:** a head or bust that still reads in a circle, as the mark page shows it.

## Asked of every brief, before it's drawn

Could you guess this character from the category alone, and could another business use it
unchanged? A brief written from what only this business has (its name, its product, its place, its
people) answers no to both; while either answer is yes, write it again from those.
