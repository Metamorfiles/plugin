# FLUX

For `flux` models (FLUX.2, klein, Kontext), reached through Black Forest Labs, fal, Replicate or Together. Read with [images.md](images.md), whose rules still hold.

- **Word order is everything.** The main subject first, then what it does, the style, the setting, the light, the technical details. What comes early wins.
- **Never write a negation.** There's no negative prompt, and "without glasses" tends to add glasses. For no text, ask for clean, blank, unmarked surfaces.
- **Brand colors by hex work** when tied to one object ("the backdrop is #1F3A2E"), not as a loose "use this color".
- **Length.** 30 to 80 words for most images. klein doesn't expand prompts, so give it fuller sentences (about 40 to 70 words).
- **Photography.** Camera, lens and film names are welcome and help realism.
- **References.** "image 1", "image 2", each with a role. The larger models take up to 8, klein 4, Kontext one. For an edit from one image, say what changes and that everything else stays as it is.
- **No transparent background**: ask for a plain backdrop.
- **Sizes** in multiples of 16, around 2 megapixels; Kontext about 1 megapixel, between 3:7 and 7:3.

Sources: Black Forest Labs' [FLUX.2 prompting guide](https://docs.bfl.ai/guides/prompting_guide_flux2) and their [prompting skills](https://github.com/black-forest-labs/skills).
